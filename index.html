Our Story

A Passion Project
Built on Love

Rosé Boutique & Wine Bar is proudly family-owned by Bob and his wife, Colleen, built from a shared love of great wine, meaningful connection, and creating a place where everyone feels welcome. What started as a dream to bring thoughtfully curated, small-production wines to Loveland has grown into a neighborhood gathering spot where wine is meant to be enjoyed — not overthought.

We believe in sip & shop: enjoy a glass from our bar or self-pour wall while browsing our hand-picked boutique of gifts, apparel, glassware, and charcuterie boards. Every bottle, every item, every experience is chosen with care, so whether you're here for a quick pour or an evening with friends, you'll always discover something special.

Rosé is more than a wine bar — it's a reflection of our family, our community, and our passion for sharing beautiful things. Welcome in, stay awhile, and make yourself at home.

— Bob & Colleen Coppola

Self-Pour Wine Machines

Designed for both casual sippers and wine enthusiasts, our self-pour machines allow you to taste a wide range of wines without the commitment of a full glass. Choose from tasting pours to explore different regions, varietals, and styles, or enjoy a full pour of your favorite selection. Each bottle is preserved using advanced technology, maintaining peak freshness and quality—making it easy to compare, learn, and discover what you love.

A Thanksgiving Feast Deserves the Perfect Wine — Our Thanksgiving Wine Tasting

Thanksgiving is all about great food, wonderful company, and, of course, the perfect wine! But with so many flavors on the holiday table, how do you know which wines to serve? Join us for one of our favorite annual traditions at Rosé Boutique & Wine Bar — our Thanksgiving Wine Tasting. We've hand-selected a delicious lineup of wines that pair beautifully with a traditional Thanksgiving dinner, served alongside Thanksgiving-inspired dishes so you can experience firsthand how the right pairing brings out the best in both your food and your wine.

Whether you're hosting the big feast, bringing a bottle to share, or simply love great wine and food, this is a tasting you won't want to miss. Gather your friends, reserve your seats, and let us help you make this Thanksgiving your most delicious yet! 🍷🦃
