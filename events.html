A Thanksgiving Feast Deserves the Perfect Wine — Our Thanksgiving Wine Tasting

Thanksgiving is all about great food, wonderful company, and, of course, the perfect wine! But with so many flavors on the holiday table, how do you know which bottle will do your turkey — and your green bean casserole, cranberry sauce, and pumpkin pie — justice?

That's exactly why we host our Thanksgiving Wine Tasting every year. Bob guided guests through a curated lineup of wines hand-selected to pair beautifully with classic holiday dishes, from crisp whites for the appetizer table to full-bodied reds for the main event and something sweet to close out the night alongside dessert. It's become one of our most beloved annual traditions — a chance for neighbors and friends to gather, taste, and get holiday-table-ready before the big day.

This year's tasting sold out quickly, and we couldn't be more grateful for the love Loveland continues to show this event. Didn't make it in this time? Don't worry — it'll be back next fall, and we'll be sharing the details first with our Instagram followers.
